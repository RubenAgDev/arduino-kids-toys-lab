ARDUINO MONSTER TRUCK CRASH DETECTOR - CIRCUIT & WIRING GUIDE
================================================================================

HARDWARE COMPONENTS REQUIRED:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 1x Arduino Uno R3 / R4 or Arduino Nano
2. 1x HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Distance Sensor (or SF-SR02)
3. 1x Buzzer (Passive Buzzer recommended for sound effects, or 5V Active Buzzer)
4. 3x LEDs (1x Green, 1x Yellow, 1x Red) - 5mm standard
5. 3x 220Ω Resistors (for LEDs)
6. 1x Breadboard & Jumper Wires (M-M / M-F)
7. Toy Monster Truck + Toy Crush Cars (or cardboard boxes/Lego blocks)

PIN CONNECTION TABLE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Component           Pin / Leg           Arduino Pin       Wire Color (Suggested)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HC-SR04 Sensor      VCC                 5V                Red
HC-SR04 Sensor      GND                 GND               Black
HC-SR04 Sensor      TRIG                Digital Pin 9     Yellow
HC-SR04 Sensor      ECHO                Digital Pin 10    Blue

Buzzer              Positive (+) / S    Digital Pin 3     Orange
Buzzer              Negative (-) / -    GND               Black

Green LED           Anode (Long Leg)    Digital Pin 4     Green (via 220Ω resistor)
Green LED           Cathode (Short Leg) GND               Black

Yellow LED          Anode (Long Leg)    Digital Pin 5     Yellow (via 220Ω resistor)
Yellow LED          Cathode (Short Leg) GND               Black

Red LED             Anode (Long Leg)    Digital Pin 6     Red (via 220Ω resistor)
Red LED             Cathode (Short Leg) GND               Black

*Note: The Arduino's built-in LED on Pin 13 will also blink automatically!

ASCII SCHEMATIC:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

           +-------------------------+
           |       ARDUINO UNO       |
           |                         |
  [5V] ----| 5V                      |
  [GND]----| GND                     |
           |                         |
           |                   Pin 9 |-----> [HC-SR04 TRIG]
           |                  Pin 10 |<----- [HC-SR04 ECHO]
           |                         |
           |                   Pin 3 |-----> (+) [BUZZER] (-) ---> GND
           |                         |
           |                   Pin 4 |-----> [220Ω] ---> [GREEN LED] ---> GND
           |                   Pin 5 |-----> [220Ω] ---> [YELLOW LED] ---> GND
           |                   Pin 6 |-----> [220Ω] ---> [RED LED] ------> GND
           +-------------------------+

PHYSICAL SETUP TRACK LAYOUT:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 [HC-SR04 SENSOR]                 [MONSTER TRUCK]              [PILE OF CARS]
       ||                                ||                          ||
       || <--------- Distance -------->  ||                          ||
       ================================================================
       0 cm                             2-4 cm                      10 cm
      (Sensor)                      (Start / Ramp)              (CRASH IMPACT!)

  1. Place the HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor securely at the starting block or ramp.
  2. The sensor's two "eyes" (Transmitter & Receiver) face down the track.
  3. Place your stack of crushable toy cars at EXACTLY 10 cm from the sensor.
  4. Place the monster truck right in front of the sensor (around 2-3 cm).
  5. Push the monster truck! As it moves AWAY from the sensor toward the pile:
     - Distance increases: 3cm -> 5cm -> 7cm -> 9cm
     - Buzzer beeps accelerate and climb in pitch!
     - LEDs flash from Green to Yellow to Red!
     - AT 10 CM: SMASH! Strobe explosion & crash alarm siren sound!
