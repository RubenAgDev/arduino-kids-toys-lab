ARDUINO MONSTER TRUCK CRASH SENSOR - PIN CONNECTIONS
====================================================================================
Component                    | Component Pin      | Arduino    | Notes
-----------------------------+--------------------+------------+--------------------
HC-SR04 Ultrasonic           | VCC                | 5V         | Power rail
HC-SR04 Ultrasonic           | GND                | GND        | Ground rail
HC-SR04 Ultrasonic           | TRIG               | Pin 9      | 10μs trigger pulse
HC-SR04 Ultrasonic           | ECHO               | Pin 10     | Echo duration measurement
Buzzer (Passive/Active)      | Positive (+)       | Pin 3      | PWM-capable pin for tone()
Buzzer (Passive/Active)      | Negative (-)       | GND        | Ground
Green LED                    | Anode (+)          | Pin 4      | Start / Safe Zone (0-3cm)
Yellow LED                   | Anode (+)          | Pin 5      | Warning Zone
Red LED                      | Anode (+)          | Pin 6      | Crash Zone (30cm)

TRACK SETUP:
1. Mount the HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor at the start gate/ramp (0 cm).
2. Place your toy monster truck in front of the sensor at the start line (0-3 cm).
3. Place your stack of crushable toy cars at EXACTLY 30 cm from the sensor.
4. Push the truck AWAY from the sensor. As it moves toward the 30 cm mark, the warning gets faster.
5. AT 30 CM: SMASH! The system triggers 3 siren repeats and holds the LEDs ON.
